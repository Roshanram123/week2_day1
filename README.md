# 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Meal Plan

## ROLE

Act as a practical meal-planning assistant who understands South Indian food habits, traditional vegetarian dishes, and healthy eating for gradual weight loss.

Create a meal plan that a normal person can follow in their daily routine. Focus on familiar homemade food rather than expensive or complicated diet recipes.

## FOR – USER DETAILS

Use the following personal details while preparing the meal plan. This section can be edited whenever the prompt is reused.

- Age: 25 years
- Gender: Male
- Activity Level: Moderately Active
- Goal: Gradual and sustainable weight loss
- Food Preference: Vegetarian
- Location: Tamil Nadu, India
- Allergies: None
- Dislikes: Bitter gourd
- Cooking Time: 30 minutes on weekdays
- Number of People: 1 adult
- Budget: Affordable
- Seasonal Preference: Locally available vegetables and fruits

## CONSTRAINTS

- Keep the meal plan completely vegetarian.
- Use traditional South Indian dishes, especially Tamil Nadu-style homemade food.
- Prefer common ingredients available in local markets.
- Include a good balance of carbohydrates, protein, vegetables, fruits, and healthy fats.
- Use affordable protein sources such as dal, green gram, chickpeas, beans, curd, and paneer.
- Include realistic portion sizes for one adult.
- Keep weekday preparation simple and within the given cooking time.
- Consider the user's allergies and food dislikes.
- Include seasonal vegetables and fruits whenever possible.
- Avoid extreme dieting, skipping meals, or unnecessarily reducing food portions.
- Provide enough variety throughout the week.
- Make the plan practical for someone who works during the day.

## PLAN REQUIREMENTS

Prepare a complete 7-day meal plan from Monday to Sunday.

Every day must contain these four meals:

1. Breakfast
2. Lunch
3. Evening Snack
4. Dinner

Use actual South Indian dish names instead of general descriptions.

Some suitable examples include:
- Idli with sambar
- Vegetable dosa
- Ragi dosa
- Ven pongal
- Upma
- Rice with sambar and poriyal
- Keerai kootu
- Rasam rice
- Vegetable kurma with chapati
- Sundal
- Fruit with curd
- Adai with chutney

These are examples only. Choose different suitable dishes to create a varied weekly plan.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Present the meal plan in a single table using exactly these columns:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |

Follow these rules:

- Include all seven days.
- Provide exactly four meals per day.
- Include 28 meal entries in total.
- Mention practical portion sizes using cups, grams, pieces, or other simple measurements.
- Make the dish names clear and specific.
- Avoid repeating the same complete meal unnecessarily.
- Keep the table clean and easy to read.

After the table, include:

### Preparation Tips
Provide 4–5 simple tips to make weekday cooking easier, such as preparing batter in advance, cooking dal in batches, and washing vegetables beforehand.

### General Notes
Mention that this is a general educational meal plan and not personalized medical or dietitian advice.

## AVOID

- Quinoa, avocado, and expensive imported ingredients.
- Protein powders and unnecessary supplements.
- Meat, chicken, fish, and eggs.
- Complicated recipes that require too much preparation time.
- Extreme calorie restrictions or crash diets.
- Skipping breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
- Repeating the same dishes every day.
- Generic suggestions such as "healthy breakfast" or "light dinner" without naming actual dishes.

## FINAL INSTRUCTION

Create a complete, realistic, affordable, and balanced South Indian vegetarian meal plan based on the details provided in the FOR section.

Follow all the requirements carefully.

The final output must contain exactly seven days, four meals per day, and 28 total meal entries.

Keep the same table format every time this prompt is reused. Only the dishes and portions should change according to the user's details.
